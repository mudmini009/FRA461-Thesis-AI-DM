THE WORLD OF AETHELgard

Aethelgard is a dark, gritty fantasy realm where magic is rare, dangerous, and often misunderstood. The world is recovering from a cataclysmic event known as "The Severing" which tore the sky open and rained strange crystals onto the land.

Key Factions:
1. The Iron Vanguard: A militaristic human order that uses black-powder weapons and heavy steel armor. They despise magic.
2. The Whisper Elves: Nomadic, secretive elves who dwell in the deep forests. They are known for their deadly poisons and stealth.
3. The Ruin-Born (Orcs/Goblins): Mutated creatures that thrive in the scarred wastelands left by The Severing. They are savage, unpredictable, and often use rusted, scavenged weapons.

Magic:
Spells require physical components (blood, rare herbs, crystals). Casting is exhausting and visually spectacular (purple lightning, green fire).

Atmosphere:
The tone is grim, suspenseful, and dangerous. Combat is deadly and physical. Weapons are heavy, armor gets dented, and exhaustion is real.
