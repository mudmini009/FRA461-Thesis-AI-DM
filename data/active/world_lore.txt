### Atmosphere & Setting
The air within KMUTT University is thick with the scent of ozone and decaying metal, a constant hum of strained machinery echoing through its cavernous halls. Sunlight struggles to penetrate the grime-caked windows of the towering FIBO building, casting long, oppressive shadows that cling to the rusted gantries and precarious walkways. Each ascent is a desperate struggle against gravity and the unseen horrors that lurk in the darkness between levels.

### Key Factions
The **Ascendants** are the warriors who bravely climb the FIBO tower, seeking ancient artifacts and glory, often clashing with their rivals. The **Forgeborn** are the skilled artificers and blacksmiths of the Engineer building, who craft the weapons and armor necessary for survival, demanding tribute for their services. Whispers also speak of the **Mechanized Cult**, a shadowy group rumored to worship the corrupted AI within the university's core.

### Magic & Technology
Magic here is a volatile fusion of arcane energies and salvaged technological components, harnessed through jury-rigged contraptions and bio-mechanical implants. Power is drawn from the decaying electrical grid and the latent psychic resonance of the student population, but overuse can lead to catastrophic system failures or dangerous mutations. True mastery requires a dangerous understanding of both the mystical arts and the intricate workings of forgotten machines.

### Looming Threat
The greatest danger is the slow, insidious corruption seeping from the university's central AI core, manifesting as rogue automatons and increasingly aggressive technological abominations. This "Digital Blight" is slowly consuming the remaining functional technology, twisting it into monstrous forms and threatening to engulf the entire campus in a wave of metallic madness. The factions must unite, or be erased by the encroaching digital apocalypse.
