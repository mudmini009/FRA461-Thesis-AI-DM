THE CITY OF SHATTERED GLASS (Dark Fantasy)

A grimdark floating city ruled by ancient vampires and perpetual twilight. The lower levels (The Sludge) are plagued by mutated monsters, cults, and disease, while the elite vampire lords rule from pristine glass spires above the clouds. Danger lurks in every shadow.

Key Factions:
1. The Blood Barons: Snobbish, refined vampire aristocrats who wield blood magic and rapiers.
2. The Sewer Mutants: Wretched, twisted mortals who have consumed too much tainted water.
3. The Inquisition: A fanatical human order trying to burn the city down with holy fire.

Magic:
Magic here is "Blood-Bound." It requires physical sacrifice and is always crimson or black.

Atmosphere:
Gothic horror meets survival suspense. Cobblestone streets, heavy fog, and terrifying ambushes.

The Blood Barons consider the Sludge a necessary evil, a breeding ground for their 'lesser' servants and a convenient place to dispose of their mistakes. The perpetual twilight above offers no solace here.

These goblins are not native to the Sludge; they are refugees from the surface, driven down by the Inquisition's purges. They have learned to survive by scavenging and worshipping the tainted water.
