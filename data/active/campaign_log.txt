[PROLOGUE]
The perpetual twilight of Shattered Glass clung to you like a shroud, the stench of decay and something metallic, like old blood, filling your nostrils. Your arcane robes, once pristine, now bore the grime of the Sludge, a testament to your relentless pursuit of forbidden knowledge. The whispers of the city, a symphony of desperation and dread, were your constant companions, but tonight, a new sound cut through the din – a guttural snarl, too close for comfort. You clutched your spell components, the crimson glow of your nascent incantation a stark contrast to the oppressive gloom.

A hulking shape detached itself from the shadows, its skin a mottled, sickly green, eyes burning with a primal hunger. The tainted waters of the Sludge had clearly been generous with their gifts, twisting this unfortunate soul into a grotesque mockery of life. It lunged, a crude, sharpened bone clutched in its clawed hand, its breath a foul miasma.
[END PROLOGUE]

[HUB] The heavy, fog-choked air of the Sludge finally gives way to the surprisingly warm, flickering lamplight of the Adventurer's Guild. The scent of stale ale and roasted meat, a stark contrast to the reek of decay you've grown accustomed to, fills your lungs. After your recent, *bloody* encounter with the cultists near the Sunken Docks, the sight of other, relatively clean adventurers sharing stories at the bar is a welcome, if temporary, reprieve. A large, battered board near the entrance is plastered with parchment, each promising coin, glory, and perhaps a chance to escape the perpetual twilight.
