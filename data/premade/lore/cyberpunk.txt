THE WORLD OF NEO-BANGKOK (Cyberpunk)

The year is 2142. The neon-drenched skies of Neo-Bangkok are choked with smog and the hum of flying Tuk-Tuks. The government was bought out decades ago by mega-corporations known as the "Zaibatsu Chao Phraya," who rule the towering glass skyscrapers, leaving the lower levels to rot in poverty and cyber-crime.

Key Factions:
1. The Chrome Syndicate: A ruthless triad that controls illegal cyberware installation in the neon slums. They use mono-kantanic katanas and high-caliber revolvers.
2. Neo-Monks: Rogue hackers who interface with the deep net in hidden server-temples. They view cyberspace as a spiritual plane and fight the corporations with devastating viruses.
3. Corp-Sec Guards (Enforcers): Heavily armored and genetically enhanced corporate police who shoot first and never ask questions.

Technology/Magic:
Magic is dead. Instead, people rely on "Chrome"—cybernetic enhancements. "Casting a spell" here means deploying a quick-hack to overheat an enemy's cyber-optics, or deploying a localized EMP grenade.

Atmosphere:
The tone is fast-paced, glowing with neon pinks and blues, but dripping with grime and desperation. Combat is brutal, involving kinetic firearms, laser blades, and frantic cover mechanics.
