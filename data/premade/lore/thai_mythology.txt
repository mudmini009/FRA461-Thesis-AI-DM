THE REALM OF HIMMAPAN (Thai Mythology Dark Fantasy)

The mystical realm of Himmapan is an ancient forest dimension where gods, demons, and legendary beasts wage an eternal shadow war. The land is defined by towering emerald jungles, golden temples crumbling into ruin, and rivers that glow with wandering spirits.

Key Factions:
1. The Yaksha (Giants): Towering, fanged demons with incredibly tough skin who wield massive golden clubs (Mace). They are territorial, aggressive, and incredibly strong.
2. The Kinnaree (Bird-Folk): Graceful, winged humanoids who dwell in the high peaks. They are masters of archery and wind magic.
3. The Krut (Garuda): Mighty eagle-warriors who wage a never-ending war against the Naga serpents. They are noble but arrogant.

Magic:
Magic in Himmapan is drawn from "Saiyasart" (dark arts) and "Buddhakhun" (sacred blessings). Spells are cast by reciting ancient Pali incantations or revealing enchanted Sak Yant (bamboo tattoos) on the caster's skin. 

Atmosphere:
The tone is mythical, grand, but intensely dangerous, filled with southeast Asian folklore. Combat involves agile martial arts (Muay Boran), enchanted curved swords (Daab), and clashes of raw elemental magic.
